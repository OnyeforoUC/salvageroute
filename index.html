    How the numbers are built
    • Resale and repair figures start from an estimated clean US retail value for the car and year, adjusted for damage and each market. They are rough; replace them with real local listings and workshop quotes.
    • Nigeria and Ghana tax a reference value for the car, not your purchase price, so cheap damaged cars still pay full-value duty. Ukraine, Georgia, Poland, the Dominican Republic and the UAE tax what you paid plus freight.
    • Poland: US-built cars pay 0% EU duty under Regulation (EU) 2026/1455; others pay 10%.
    • Rates marked "verify" come from a single secondary source. Rates and exchange rates change often. This is not financial, legal or tax advice.
